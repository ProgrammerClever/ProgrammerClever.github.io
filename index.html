About Me

Get to know the person behind the code and the camera.

Mukunzi Clever is a 19-year-old Rwanda-based Software Development Engineer and Model, born on 28 August 2007. He builds modern, scalable full-stack web applications and database systems. He is currently based in Kigali, Rwanda, and also has roots in Huye, Rwanda.

Alongside developing practical digital platforms—such as School ERP, Tourism Management, and Library Management systems—he works in fashion, portraiture, and visual storytelling to craft strong personal and commercial brands.

He attended Faith Preparatory Academy, Groupe Scolaire Mere de Verbe, and currently studies at Lycée de Nyanza. His core technical skills include HTML, CSS, JavaScript, PHP, Python, MySQL, MongoDB, and Git.

Mukunzi Clever is the child of Uwayezu Angelique and has 2 siblings. In his free time, he builds digital art, photography, and blogs just for fun and to test new ideas. He is an avid fan of PES2026 and GTA VI, enjoys music by Don Toliver and Chris Brown, and his favorite tool is his camera. His current daily driver is an iPhone 13 Pro Max.
